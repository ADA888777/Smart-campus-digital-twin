    Events and alerts

      This dashboard is a digital twin: a software model of the Packet Tracer topology, not a live connection to it. Latency, packet loss and availability are simulated values produced by the twin engine from the modelled device state, not measurements read from Packet Tracer or from real hardware. Scenario actions change the state of this twin and its own event, alert and fault records only; no Packet Tracer device is modified.
